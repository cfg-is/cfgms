CFGMSsubject-role assignment
—

Subject-role assignment (Issue #3134) — extends AccountsView's account row with an expand-row (the same pattern RolesView uses for permissions), so an admin never leaves Accounts to see or change what roles a subject holds. Click a row to expand it, use the role picker + Assign to grant a role, the × chip to open a revoke confirm. Click Assign on fleet-admin's operator row to see the 403 escalation-prevention message (distinct from a generic failure). Both themes.
